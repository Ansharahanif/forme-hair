The Forme notes

Small tips, better hair days.

Thoughtful basics for a routine that works with your hair, not against it.

01 / PREP

Start with completely dry hair.

Heat tools work best on dry hair. Let your hair air-dry or blow-dry it first, then brush through gently to remove tangles.

02 / PROTECT

Make heat protection a habit.

Apply a heat-protectant product evenly before styling. Follow its directions and let it dry before reaching for a hot tool.

03 / SECTION

Work in manageable sections.

Smaller sections give you more control and an even finish. Clip away the rest so you can work from underneath to the top.

04 / TEMPERATURE

Use the lowest useful heat.

Start low and adjust only if needed. Fine or fragile hair usually needs less heat than thick, coarse hair.

05 / TECHNIQUE

Keep the tool moving.

Glide steadily without stopping in one place. For curls, wrap each section smoothly and release rather than pulling at the hair.

06 / CARE

Give your tools a little care.

Unplug and cool tools completely before cleaning. Wipe plates or barrels with a soft, dry cloth and store safely.
