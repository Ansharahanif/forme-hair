Good to know

Frequently asked questions

A few answers to help you feel good about your tools and your routine.

How do I choose the right styling tool?

Start with the finish you reach for most. A straightener can smooth and create a soft bend, while a curler is designed for more defined movement. Visit each collection page for details.

Can I use a hot tool on damp hair?

No. Always use hot styling tools on fully dry hair unless the tool is specifically designed for wet-to-dry styling. Check the product instructions before use.

What temperature should I use?

Start with the lowest setting that works for your hair. Fine or chemically treated hair generally needs less heat; thicker textures may need more. Use heat protection every time.

How should I clean my styling tools?

Unplug the tool and let it cool completely. Wipe the surfaces with a soft, dry cloth. Do not immerse an electrical styling tool in water.

Do you offer a warranty?

Warranty terms vary by product and region. Please keep your proof of purchase and contact our team with the name of your tool for help.
